Questions? Fair.

What is Witchstream?

A player for your TV and phone that makes your own sources look and feel like a proper streaming app. Home screen, profiles, Continue watching, the works.

How much is it?

Nothing. No ads, no subscription, no account with us. If you love it, you can buy me a coffee in crypto.

Does it come with films and shows?

No. Witchstream comes empty. You add your own sources: Stremio compatible addons, your own media server, or TV playlists you're allowed to use. What you add is up to you.

Why do I have to set things up?

Because we don't host anything. No servers, no accounts, nothing of yours on our side. That's why it's free and private. The trade: a few features use free services in your own name. It takes about 10 minutes, once. Setup guide

What works with no setup at all?

Add a source and you're watching: home screen, search, profiles, Continue watching, downloads, sleep timer, Play on TV. Everything below is a bonus.

What's the TMDB key for?

TMDB is a free film database. Your own key unlocks personal home rows, "Because you watched", actor and collection pages, cast and crew in the player, and search by actor or director. Free account, about 2 minutes. How to get one

What's Firebase and do I need it?

Only if you use more than one device. Firebase is Google's free service, and your own project keeps profiles, lists and progress in sync between your TV and phone. No card needed, about 5 minutes on a computer. Without it, everything still works on each device. Set up sync

Do I need to pay for anything?

Not for Witchstream. Some sources or services you choose to add may cost money, like a debrid service for faster streams. That's between you and them.

Where can I watch?

Android TV, Google TV and Android phones. Your lists and progress can follow you between them with sync.

Do you see what I watch?

No. There's nothing on our side to send it to. Everything stays on your devices, or in your own sync project if you turn sync on. Privacy policy
